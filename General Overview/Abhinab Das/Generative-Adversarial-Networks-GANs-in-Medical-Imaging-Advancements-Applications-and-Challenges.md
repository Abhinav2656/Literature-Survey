## Type of Paper
Survey

## Authors
Arvind Singh, Gaurav Maheshwari, Anuj Kumar, Vimal Gupta

## Citation
A. Singh, G. Maheshwari, A. Kumar, and V. Gupta, "Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in Medical Imaging: Advancements, Applications, and Challenges," *J. Imaging Inform. Med.*, vol. 37, no. 2, pp. 781–795, 2024, doi: [10.1007/s10278-023-01121-3](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378499876_Generative_Adversarial_Networks_GANs_in_Medical_Imaging_Advancements_Applications_and_Challenges).

## Description
This survey explores advancements in GANs for medical imaging, covering applications like synthesis, segmentation, and denoising, and discussing challenges like training stability.

## Methodology
Reviews GAN variants (e.g., CycleGAN, StyleGAN) and their applications, analyzing performance and limitations in medical imaging tasks.

## Training & Dataset
Not applicable (survey paper). References datasets like CheXpert, ISIC, and BraTS.

## Limitations
GANs face issues like mode collapse and require extensive computational resources.

## Advantages
Provides a detailed guide to GAN applications, aiding researchers and clinicians.

## Applications
Image synthesis, segmentation, denoising, and anomaly detection in medical imaging.

## Evaluation Metrics
Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Dice score, Sensitivity.
