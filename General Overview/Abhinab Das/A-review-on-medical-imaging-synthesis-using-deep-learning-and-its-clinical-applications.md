## Type of Paper
Survey

## Authors
Tonghe Wang, Yang Lei, Yabo Fu, James J. Wynne, Walter J. Curran, Tian Liu, Xiaofeng Yang

## Citation
T. Wang, Y. Lei, Y. Fu, J. J. Wynne, W. J. Curran, T. Liu, and X. Yang, "A review on medical imaging synthesis using deep learning and its clinical applications," *J. Appl. Clin. Med. Phys.*, vol. 22, no. 11, pp. 208–233, 2021, doi: [10.1002/acm2.13403](https://doi.org/10.1002/acm2.13403).

## Description
This survey reviews deep learning-based medical image synthesis, focusing on GANs and their clinical applications in MRI, CT, and PET imaging.

## Methodology
Analyzes deep learning models, primarily GANs, for image synthesis, comparing architectures and clinical outcomes across modalities.

## Training & Dataset
Not applicable (survey paper). References datasets like TCIA, BraTS, and LIDC-IDRI.

## Limitations
Synthetic images require clinical validation; GAN training can be unstable.

## Advantages
Highlights clinical potential of synthetic images, improving diagnostic workflows.

## Applications
Image synthesis for cross-modality translation, data augmentation, and radiotherapy planning.

## Evaluation Metrics
Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR), Structural Similarity Index (SSIM).
