## Type of Paper
Survey / Empirical Study

## Authors
Youssef Skandarani, Pierre-Marc Jodoin, Alain Lalande

## Citation
Y. Skandarani, P.-M. Jodoin, and A. Lalande, "Generative Adversarial Networks for Medical Image Synthesis: An Empirical Study," *J. Imaging Inform. Med.*, vol. 36, no. 4, pp. 1701–1714, 2021, doi: [10.1007/s10278-021-00476-9](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/351537445_GANs_for_Medical_Image_Synthesis_An_Empirical_Study).

## Description
This empirical study evaluates GANs for medical image synthesis, focusing on their performance in generating realistic images for tasks like segmentation and classification.

## Methodology
Compares GAN architectures (e.g., DCGAN, StyleGAN) using a standardized framework, assessing image quality and utility for downstream tasks.

## Training & Dataset
Datasets include CheXpert, ISIC 2018, and BraTS 2018. Training used NVIDIA GPUs with varying configurations.

## Limitations
Some GANs struggle with high-resolution images; results vary by dataset.

## Advantages
Offers a benchmark for GAN performance, guiding model selection for medical imaging.

## Applications
Image synthesis for data augmentation, segmentation, and classification.

## Evaluation Metrics
Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Precision, Recall, F1-score.
