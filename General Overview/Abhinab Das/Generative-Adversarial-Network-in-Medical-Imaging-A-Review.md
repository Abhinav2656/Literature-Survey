## Type of Paper
Survey

## Authors
Xin Yi, Ekta Walia, Paul Babyn

## Citation
X. Yi, E. Walia, and P. Babyn, "Generative Adversarial Network in Medical Imaging: A Review," *Med. Image Anal.*, vol. 58, p. 101552, 2019, doi: [10.1016/j.media.2019.101552](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2019.101552).

## Description
This survey reviews GAN applications in medical imaging, covering image synthesis, segmentation, and reconstruction, highlighting their potential and challenges.

## Methodology
Analyzes GAN architectures (e.g., DCGAN, CycleGAN) and their applications in medical imaging tasks, comparing performance and methodologies.

## Training & Dataset
Not applicable (survey paper). References datasets like BraTS, CheXpert, and ADNI.

## Limitations
GAN training instability and mode collapse remain challenges; clinical adoption requires validation.

## Advantages
Provides a comprehensive overview of GAN applications, guiding future research.

## Applications
Image synthesis, segmentation, reconstruction, and denoising in medical imaging.

## Evaluation Metrics
Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Dice score, Mean Absolute Error (MAE).
