## Type of Paper
Survey

## Authors
Zhe Wang, Lei Zhang, Yibo Fan, Jiaxi Yang

## Citation
Z. Wang, L. Zhang, Y. Fan, and J. Yang, "A Comprehensive Survey for Generative Data Augmentation," *arXiv preprint*, 2024, doi: [10.48550/arXiv.2405.14421](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/380635431_A_Comprehensive_Survey_on_Data_Augmentation).

## Description
This survey reviews generative data augmentation techniques, including GANs, VAEs, and diffusion models, for medical imaging, focusing on their role in addressing data scarcity.

## Methodology
Categorizes generative models by architecture and application, analyzing their effectiveness in data augmentation for tasks like classification and segmentation.

## Training & Dataset
Not applicable (survey paper). References datasets like CheXpert, BraTS, and ADNI.

## Limitations
Generative models may introduce artifacts, and their effectiveness depends on downstream task compatibility.

## Advantages
Provides a broad perspective on generative augmentation, aiding researchers in method selection.

## Applications
Data augmentation for medical imaging tasks, including classification, segmentation, and synthesis.

## Evaluation Metrics
Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), classification accuracy, Dice score.
