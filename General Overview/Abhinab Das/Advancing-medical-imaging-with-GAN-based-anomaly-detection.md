## Type of Paper
Survey

## Authors
Mohammed Al-Shami, Abdulla Alshamsi, Hwee Kuan Lee, Muhammad Naseer Bajwa

## Citation
M. Al-Shami, A. Alshamsi, H. K. Lee, and M. N. Bajwa, "Advancing medical imaging with GAN-based anomaly detection," *J. Imaging Inform. Med.*, vol. 38, no. 3, pp. 1396–1409, 2025, doi: [10.1007/s10278-024-01201-y](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381868793_Advancing_medical_imaging_with_GAN-based_anomaly_detection).

## Description
This survey reviews GAN-based anomaly detection in medical imaging, covering models like BiGAN, AnoGAN, GANomaly, Skip-GANomaly, GAAL, MadGAN, and RandGAN, focusing on their applications and challenges.

## Methodology
Analyzes various GAN architectures for anomaly detection, comparing their mechanisms (e.g., encoder-decoder in GANomaly, bidirectional mapping in BiGAN) and performance in unsupervised settings.

## Training & Dataset
Not applicable (survey paper). References datasets like BraTS, ISIC, and CheXpert used in reviewed studies.

## Limitations
GAN-based anomaly detection struggles with subtle anomalies and requires high-quality training data.

## Advantages
Comprehensive overview of GAN models, guiding researchers in selecting appropriate methods for anomaly detection.

## Applications
Anomaly detection in MRI, CT, and X-ray for identifying diseases like tumors or lesions.

## Evaluation Metrics
Area Under the Curve (AUC), Sensitivity, Specificity.
