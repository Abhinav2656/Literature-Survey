## Description
This paper explores GAN variants (CGAN, CTGAN, CRAMER GAN, DRAGAN, WGAN) for generating synthetic medical tabular data, addressing data privacy and scarcity.

## Methodology
Compares CGAN, CTGAN, CRAMER GAN, DRAGAN, and WGAN, focusing on their ability to generate realistic tabular data using conditional and Wasserstein losses.

## Training & Dataset
Trained on medical tabular datasets (e.g., MIMIC-III, synthetic EHR data). Training used NVIDIA A100 GPUs.

## Limitations
Generated data may not fully capture complex correlations in tabular data; requires careful validation.

## Advantages
Addresses privacy concerns and data scarcity, enabling robust machine learning models.

## Applications
Synthetic tabular data generation for healthcare analytics and model training.

## Evaluation Metrics
Jensen-Shannon Divergence (JSD), Wasserstein Distance.
